# Cold-palette component review

The latest user reference shows seven frontal mechanical sprites. Its cold steel and orange copper
replace the previous sage/brass treatment. Use navy contours, compact highlights and clear functional
parts; avoid diagonal views that obscure the shafts, gearing or winding at 16px.

1. Palette and form pass: restored frontal construction with a structural frame, a two-gear drive,
   a motor with copper winding and feet, tapered cutting heads, a valve manifold and a PCB.
   Steel uses five blue-gray values with a sixth, darker navy value for the silhouette.
2. Readability pass: inspected at 8x and 4x on inventory gray. Kept the frame aperture open,
   the two drive shafts perpendicular, the motor winding in three broad copper strips, and the
   manifold branches separated. Cyan segments distinguish the reinforced head.
3. Export review: native 16x16 PNGs and two-layer Aseprite masters retain their existing IDs.
   The silhouette layer supplies a 1px navy contour beneath the material/working-part layer.
   Binary transparency and a clear canvas margin are checked with Aseprite.

The workbench model, UV texture and vanilla-style interface are unchanged.
